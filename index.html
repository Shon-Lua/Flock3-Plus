Flock3+

Multimedia player

Install GitHub About
Flock3+ is a portable multimedia player that supports downloading tracks over the Internet, playing MP3 and WAV files. An indispensable gadget for audiophiles and DIY electronics enthusiasts.

READ BEFORE INSTALLING

  1. Connect the LILYGO T5-4.7-S3 Pro board to USB.
  2. Click Connect and select the correct COM port.
  3. Click the Install button and wait for the download to finish. (Never disconnect the cable during downloading, otherwise flash memory may be damaged.)
  4. During flashing, try to stay on the download page, otherwise transfer speed may decrease.
  5. After the download is complete, disconnect the board from USB and enjoy a high-quality MP3 player.
