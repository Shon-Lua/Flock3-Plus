READ BEFORE INSTALLING

  1. Connect the LILYGO T5-4.7-S3 Pro board to USB.
  2. Click Connect and select the correct COM port.
  3. Click the Install button and wait for the download to finish. (Never disconnect the cable during downloading, otherwise flash memory may be damaged.)
  4. During flashing, try to stay on the download page, otherwise transfer speed may decrease.
  5. After the download is complete, disconnect the board from USB and enjoy a high-quality MP3 player.

About Flock3+

Flock3+ is a versatile multimedia player designed for the LILYGO T5-4.7-S3 Pro board. The firmware provides a rich set of features that turn the board into a full-featured music player.

ID3v2 Tag Support

The player includes a custom library for extracting ID3v2 tags from MP3 files and displaying them on the e-ink screen. This allows you to see track titles, artist names, album names, and album art (if available) directly on the display.

Internet Search and Streaming

Flock3+ can search for tracks over the internet, allowing you to find and play music online without needing to store files locally. It supports streaming from various online sources and can download tracks for offline playback.

Bluetooth Audio Output

Bluetooth audio output is fully supported. You can pair wireless speakers or headphones and enjoy your music without cables. The player acts as a Bluetooth transmitter, streaming audio to any compatible device.

Backlight Control

The device features adjustable backlight control for the display, so you can set the brightness according to your environment. This is especially useful in low-light conditions or outdoors.

Landscape Orientation

Flock3+ also supports landscape orientation, giving you the flexibility to use the player in a horizontal layout. This is ideal for album art display and a more traditional media player interface.

Customization

Customization is a key aspect of the firmware. You can modify the user interface, change color schemes, adjust playback settings, and even add new functionality through configuration files or by editing the source code.
